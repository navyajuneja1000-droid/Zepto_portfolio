# zepto × navya

Navya Juneja's product portfolio for Zepto PM roles, built to feel like shopping on a quick-commerce app.

- **Home**: "Hire a PM in 10 minutes" banner, offer strip, Shop by Category tiles, Bestsellers and PhonePe Essentials rails, a Super Saver rail that filters by evidence, and a bill-details calculator that follows the money.
- **Categories** (`#/cn/<category>`): every case study on a shelf, with a left category rail, filters (company, evidence) and sort.
- **Product pages** (`#/pn/<id>`): gallery, green price tag (the case's headline number), Highlights, Product description, Nutritional information (every metric labelled), How it was made, Why this matters at Zepto, Similar products.
- **PhonePe Store** (`#/brand/phonepe`): the two years at PhonePe as a brand store.
- **Orders** (`#/orders`): experience and education as past orders. **Cart** collects cases; Place Order opens an interview-request email. The cart is saved in the browser only.
- **Account** (`#/account`): contact, how I work, off the clock, Help & Support (FAQ).

Every figure is labelled Result, Built, Modelled, Target or Sourced.

Static site: open `index.html` or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`). Content lives in `assets/data.js`.

Independent job-application portfolio. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Zepto or PhonePe.
